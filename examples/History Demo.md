## Why

A small build I can finish in two days with tools I already own. The birdhouse goes on the fence by the kitchen window, so the hole faces east, away from the weather.

I would rather build one good one than three rough ones.

## Materials

1. One cedar board, 1 x 6, six feet
2. Wood glue and 1¼" exterior screws
3. Sandpaper, 120 grit
4. A hinge for the cleaning door

## Budget

- Cedar board: $14
- Screws and glue: $9
- Hinge: $4
- Total: about $27

## Schedule

- **Saturday morning:** cut the pieces and sand the edges
- **Saturday afternoon:** assemble, let the glue set overnight
- **Sunday:** hang the door, drill the hole, mount it on the fence

## Notes

The entrance hole should be 1½" across, about six inches above the floor. No perch; it only helps predators. Drill drainage holes in the floor and leave the roof overhanging so rain runs off.
